## Stable Facts
Context ID: FACTS-001
Confidence: High; verified against commit `68f3e7a`.
- Base-16 instructions are 32 bits; data words are 16 bits and word-addressed.
- v1.0 direct memory operands are `{mmio, addr11}` and address 2048 words in
  each of two non-aliasing spaces.
- `MVF`/`MVT` encode `mmio` at bit 21 and `addr11` at bits 20:10.
- `MSI` encodes `mmio` at bit 27, `addr11` at bits 26:16, and `imm16` at
  bits 15:0.
- `tables/opcode-map.csv` and `tables/encoding-fields.csv` are canonical inputs;
  instruction pages are generated by `tools/generate_instruction_docs.py`.

## Decisions
Context ID: DECISIONS-001
Confidence: High; user direction recorded 2026-08-16.
- v1.0 is the compatibility boundary after v0.x.
- `MVF`, `MVT`, and `MSI` must explicitly select ordinary memory or MMIO before
  their address.
- MMIO supplies an architecture-defined peripheral space so portable peripheral
  access does not depend on vendor-defined opcodes.
- `MVF`, `MVT`, and `MSI` uniformly use `{mmio, addr11}` for 2048 directly
  addressable words in each space.
- `MSI` remains one 32-bit word. Bit 27 is `mmio`, bits 26:16 are `addr11`, and
  bits 15:0 remain `imm16`; internal staging is not architecturally observable.
- Base-16T full pointers use `{mmio, addr15}`; `r59` is
  `sap`, `r60` is `sdata`; ordinary memory `0x0000`-`0x001F` is the default
  configurable scratch block.
- `MCP [sap], mem:[scratch]` is opcode `00:1100` and explicitly copies an
  ordinary-memory scratch word to a register-addressed memory/MMIO destination.
- OASIS-32 preserves separate memory/MMIO spaces through an M-format space bit
  while retaining full 32-bit addresses and requiring no baseline scratch area.
